Automatic Labeling

Each template automatically applies labels based on your selections. Here's how the labeling works:

💡 How it works: When you check boxes in the templates, the action automatically detects these selections and applies the corresponding labels to your issue.

Story Point Labels

Estimate the effort required for the task:

⏳ Story Points: 1 - 1-2 hours ⏳ Story Points: 2-3 - 2-4 hours ⏳ Story Points: 5 - 5-8 hours ⏳ Story Points: 8 - 10-16 hours ⏳ Story Points: 13 - 16+ hours ⏳ Story Points: 20+ - 40+ hours

Priority Labels

Set the urgency of the task:

🚨 Priority: Critical - Must handle immediately 🚨 Priority: High - Requires timely attention 🚨 Priority: Medium - Important but not urgent 🚨 Priority: Low - Minimal impact

Customizing Templates

You can customize the templates to match your team's workflow and requirements:

# config.yml - Customize template sections templates: feature_request: sections: - name: "Feature Description" required: true type: "text" - name: "User Story" required: false type: "text" - name: "Acceptance Criteria" required: true type: "checklist" - name: "Story Points" required: true type: "radio" options: - "1" - "2-3" - "5" - "8" - "13" - "20+"

You can also add custom sections, modify the checkbox options, and change the template structure to match your specific needs.

Template Best Practices

For Template Usage

  • Choose the right template: Use the most appropriate template for your issue type
  • Fill out all required fields: Complete all mandatory sections for better labeling
  • Be specific: Provide detailed information to help with estimation and prioritization
  • Use checkboxes consistently: Only check the most appropriate options
  • Update existing issues: Edit issues to add missing information using templates

For Template Customization

  • Keep it simple: Don't add too many fields that might discourage usage
  • Make it relevant: Customize templates for your specific project needs
  • Test with your team: Get feedback on template usability
  • Iterate: Continuously improve templates based on usage patterns

For Team Adoption

  • Train your team: Show everyone how to use the templates effectively
  • Lead by example: Use templates consistently in your own issues
  • Provide feedback: Encourage team members to suggest improvements
  • Monitor usage: Track how well templates are being adopted
