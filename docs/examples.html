Best Practices from Real Teams

💡 Start Small: Begin with basic story points and priority labels, then gradually add more sophisticated detection rules as your team becomes comfortable with the system.
💡 Team Training: Conduct a 30-minute training session to show your team how to use the templates effectively. Include examples of good vs. bad issue descriptions.
💡 Regular Reviews: Schedule monthly reviews to analyze labeling accuracy and adjust detection rules based on team feedback.
⚠️ Avoid Over-Engineering: Don't create too many labels or complex detection rules initially. Focus on the most important categorizations for your workflow.
⚠️ Monitor Performance: Keep an eye on GitHub API rate limits and adjust the RATE_LIMIT_DELAY if you experience issues with large repositories.
